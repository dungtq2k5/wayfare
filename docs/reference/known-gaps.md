# Known Gaps — Read Before You Trip Over Them

**Current state.** Real, current, and they bite silently. Each row names the file that proves it. And when a gap is closed, just add ***Closed.*** to indicate it's closed.

| # | Gap | Where | Consequence |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | The selected marker's name chip can render under the Place sheet, behind the mini player, or over the area-switcher pill | `apps/mobile`, the map's selected `Marker` (MapLibre React Native's native annotation ordering) | At the sheet's half height, or near the top of the map, the selected Place's name is partly hidden; the sheet itself still names it |
| 2 | The device voice (audio tier 3, offline) shows no media card on the lock screen or in the notification shade | `apps/mobile/src/player/` — `expo-speech` has no player for `expo-audio`'s lock-screen support to attach to | Offline, a narration in the phone's own voice can only be paused or stopped from inside the app |
| 3 | Dismissing a paused narration's notification does not stop it | `expo-audio` 57 does not report the dismissal | The narration stays paused in the app, with its mini player, until the tourist stops or resumes it there |
| 4 | The walk's notification has no *Stop walk* action and no running time; its text is fixed for the whole walk ("Tap to see your walk or stop it.") | `expo-location`'s foreground-service notification takes a title, a body and a colour only, and Android refuses to restart the service from the background, so its text cannot follow the walk there | To stop a walk, or to see its count and any problem, the tourist opens the app (one tap on the notification) |
| 5 | Map markers have no per-marker screen-reader label ("{Place}, heard" is on Explore's rows only) | `apps/mobile/src/map/` — markers are MapLibre symbols, not views | A screen-reader user finds heard Places through Explore, not the map |
| 6 | Wayfare's links (`go.wayfare.app/q/…`, `wayfare.app/p/…`) open the browser, not the app | No `/.well-known/assetlinks.json` is served on either host yet, so Android never verifies the app's link claims | A sticker scanned with the phone's own camera, or a link in a message, opens a web page that does not exist yet; the in-app scanner is unaffected. A test phone needs `pm set-app-links-user-selection` (architecture §4, *Deep links*) |
| 7 | The Japanese, Chinese (Simplified) and Korean UI bundles are not committed: only `en` and `vi` are in `packages/i18n/locales/`, so the launch languages' screens are machine-translated whole, and no native reader has seen them | `packages/i18n/locales/` (architecture §4 says the launch locales are committed) | The screens, and the switch's confirmation, read as machine translation; locally the fake translator shows "[ja] …". Commit reviewed `ja`, `zh-Hans` and `ko` bundles before launch |
| 8 | The privacy policy, the terms and the owner agreement are drafts: the pages exist, the text is not the product owner's yet | `apps/web/content/legal/` (English and Vietnamese, each marked draft) | Every app and the console link to text that isn't final; a version accepted today names a draft |
| 9 | A walk the phone stops is not restarted: the OS wake-up net of ADR 0026 (geofence regions that revive the walk) is not wired | `packages/core/src/geofence/wake-up.ts` (`wakeUpRegions`) exists; nothing in `apps/mobile` calls it or `startGeofencingAsync` | If Android kills the location task, the walk stays dead until the tourist opens the app, which then says the phone stopped it |
| 10 | ***Closed.*** **A Place confirmed on the same fix as another, or while ineligible (its cooldown, the Venue window), never fires while the tourist stays inside** — the engine's `reconcile` runs only on a weak fix or while a refused offer waits, never on a timer, and `safetyReconcileIntervalMs` is read by no app code; the engine's own specs pass only because their `replay()` adds the reconcile the app doesn't | `apps/mobile/src/walk/handler.ts` (`handleFix`); `packages/core/src/geofence/engine.ts` | Two Places at one spot: the second is never told; a capped Venue never fires once its window reopens. Fixed: after a fix that decides nothing, `handleFix` reconciles whenever a confirmed Place is still waiting (`hasWaitingConfirmed`), paced by `safetyReconcileIntervalMs` |
| 11 | A language switch discards the old language's on-demand requests instead of cancelling them | `apps/mobile/src/player/ladder.ts` (`stale()`); conventions §12.3 says cancel | The requests still run and may still create server work; their results are dropped, never played |
| 12 | A few calls stall about 2 s and miss the gateway's deadline — 1 to 3 per `screens` suite (seen on WQ1's first read after setup, AD3, Z1b, C8b) | cause not found; five others were found and fixed (lazy gRPC channels, pools closing idle connections, disk-flush stalls, a swapping machine, a nightly job mid-run) — each failure shows as a ~2 s span in its trace.
