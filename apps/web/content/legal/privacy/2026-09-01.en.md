# Privacy policy

## 1. What Wayfare collects

When you open Wayfare without an account, your browser gets an anonymous device number. We keep which places it opened and which stories it played, to count listens and improve the stories. We never keep where you are.

## 2. With an account

Your name, email and saved places. You can delete them at any time from your account page.
